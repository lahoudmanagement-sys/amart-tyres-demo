About us

A family-owned shop that does the job properly.

Amart Tyres is a family-owned tyre and wheel shop on Parramatta Road in Granville. We fit tyres, align wheels and service cars with computerised alignment equipment, digital balancing machines and proper diagnostic tools.

We ask what you drive and how you drive it before we recommend anything. Small car, SUV, 4WD, performance car or work truck, you get honest advice and a fair price.

Common questions

Before you visit.

Do I need to book?

No. You can drive in during opening hours. If you call ahead or send a quote request, we can check stock and have your tyres ready so you wait less.

Do new tyres come with a warranty?

Yes. Every new tyre we sell is covered by a warranty. The terms depend on the brand and type of tyre, and we'll explain them when you buy.

How do I find my tyre size?

Look at the side of your tyre for a code like 205/55 R16. Enter those three numbers in the quote form. If you can't find it, tell us your car's make, model and year and we'll look it up.

What vehicles do you work on?

All makes and models of cars, SUVs and 4WDs, plus light and heavy-duty trucks and fleet vehicles.

How can I pay?

Cash, EFTPOS, Visa and Mastercard.
